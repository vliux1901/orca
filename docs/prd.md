# Product feature requirements

## Context

On our daily lives, we constantly receive paper or digital documents (Eg,
receipts). But after
a while they are spread all over the mailbox or file systems, making it super
difficult to:

- find all the documents for the same subject (Eg, for your car, or your house)
- search through all the documents to find the all information about the
  subject. Eg, what maintenances / repairs have been done since I bought this
  car 5 years ago?
- give answers based on the information provided. Eg, dealer says I should
  replace the brake fluid. Can you tell me whether I should do it based on:
    - when / the mileage that car's brake fluid was replaced last time
    - and the manufacture's recommended replacement intervals based on time /
      mileage.

Although Telsa auto's owners can query the maintenance records from the mobile
app, it looks like they can only access PDF files which may not contain enough
details to answer those questions.
